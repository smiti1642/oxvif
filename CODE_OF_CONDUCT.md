# Code of conduct

oxvif contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, technically honest,
and harassment-free community.

We welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or
commercial affiliation. Constructive participation includes focusing criticism
on code and evidence, stating uncertainty, correcting mistakes openly,
protecting device-owner privacy, and giving others room to ask questions.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
misrepresentation, publishing another person's private information, or using
project channels for unwanted commercial solicitation are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily limit,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior is unsafe or persistently
disruptive. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior
behavior, and the safety of affected people. Report conduct concerns privately
through the repository's security-advisory channel when public discussion would
expose personal information. Good-faith reports will be handled discreetly and
without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
